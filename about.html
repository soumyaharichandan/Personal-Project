the story

I'm a self-taught designer — the foundation was laid one hard lesson at a time, through continuous learning rather than a classroom. That same restlessness pushed me to start my own company early on, and I saw it through to a successful exit.

Since then I've worked across corporates in different design capacities — always close to the problem, never far from the people building the product. Alongside that, I've helped a handful of startups design their very first products from a blank screen, because that's still the work I enjoy most.
