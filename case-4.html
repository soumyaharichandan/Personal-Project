Case study · Enterprise reporting

How I turned a 5-hour reporting workflow into 45 minutes.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

3 months

Team

PM, 3 engineers

Outcome

5h → 45m

The problem

Building a client report meant stitching data from five screens by hand, every month.

Approach

Timed the workflow with real users and removed every step that existed only for the system.

The solution

One guided flow that pulls the data in, flags gaps, and exports a ready-to-send report.

Results & reflections

Report time fell from hours to under an hour with fewer errors (demo numbers).
