most of the work has been zero-to-one. just a problem, a constraint, and a deadline.

every project starts the same way — not with a screen, but a question: who is this actually for, and what's being missed? the real business need rarely shows up in the kickoff; it surfaces after talking to the people who'll use the thing and chasing the edge cases nobody mentioned. the next question is technical: how is this actually built, and where will it break under real use — a conversation with engineering, had before anything gets sketched. design starts only once both the people and the machine are understood: for speed, for clarity, for the moment it all just makes sense. that's the process i try to follow.
