Case study · Security SaaS

I redesigned how analysts collaborate with AI during investigations.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

4 months

Team

PM, 5 engineers, 2 researchers

Outcome

Faster triage

The problem

Analysts were copy-pasting between an AI chat and their case tool, losing context every time.

Approach

Shadowed analysts, mapped the investigation loop, and found that trust, not speed, was the blocker.

The solution

AI suggestions now live inside the case timeline, with sources shown and every step reversible.

Results & reflections

Median triage time dropped and analysts began using the assistant on most cases (demo numbers).
