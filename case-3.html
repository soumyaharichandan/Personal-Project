Case study · Design system

How a new UI kit made dark mode and white-labeling possible.

Role

Staff Product Designer

Timeline

5 months

Team

3 designers, 6 engineers

Outcome

1 kit, N brands

The problem

Hard-coded colours and one-off components made theming impossible and slowed every release.

Approach

Audited every component, defined semantic tokens, and migrated product areas in phases.

The solution

A token-based kit with light, dark and white-label themes, documented with usage guidance.

Results & reflections

Dark mode shipped, new brands launched without redesign, and design-to-dev handoff got faster (demo numbers).
