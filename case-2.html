Case study · Partner portal

How UX design unlocked $5,000 in credits for partners.

Role

Senior Product Designer

Timeline

6 weeks

Team

PM, 2 engineers

Outcome

+ eligible partners

The problem

Partners missed a credits programme because eligibility was buried in a long document.

Approach

Rewrote the eligibility flow as a short checklist with live status and clear next steps.

The solution

A guided checklist replaced the PDF, with progress saved so partners could return later.

Results & reflections

More partners completed eligibility, and support tickets on the topic fell (demo numbers).
